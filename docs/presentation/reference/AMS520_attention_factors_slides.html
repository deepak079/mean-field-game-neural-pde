Three identifiers

  • PERMNO: a share class in CRSP, for life. Travelers kept 59459 through five tickers.
  • PERMCO: the company in CRSP. GOOGL and GOOG share one, and both get Alphabet’s accounts.
  • GVKEY: the company in Compustat. Apple: PERMNO 14593, GVKEY 1690.

No look-ahead

  • Universe of month t: the top 500 at the end of month t − 1.
  • Accounting data wait 6 months after the fiscal year ends.
  • Characteristics are known at the close of day t − 1.

Conventions

  • CRSP in CIZ format, to Dec 2025 (the legacy format stops in Dec 2024).
  • CRSP values in $ thousands, Compustat in $ millions.
  • Missing characteristics: last observed value, else the cross-sectional median.

Arbitrage trading · costs

Every trade has a cost

cost(ωt, ωt−1)= 0.0005 ‖ωt − ωt−1‖15 bp per unit traded + 0.0001 ‖max(−ωt, 0)‖11 bp per unit short

Rportt,net=Rportt−cost(ωt, ωt−1)

Costs are paid every day on ω, so the model has to learn to trade less, not only to predict better.

Arbitrage trading · objective

One objective trains the whole chain

maxWK, Q, θ SRnetSharpe ratio after costs + λVar · EVexplained variance s.t. ‖ωt‖1 = 1
SRnet=mean(Rnet) − Rfstd(Rnet) EV=1NΣi (1 − Var(εi)Var(Ri))

Sharpe after costs

Costs depend on ωt and ωt−1, so the model is pushed toward steady portfolios with few shorts.

Explained variance · λVar = 100

Pins down the factors: without it, many different ωF would give the same Sharpe ratio.

Fixed leverage · ‖ωt‖₁ = 1

The Sharpe ratio cannot be pushed up just by betting more.
